A single original one-line joke about office life, written for make.fun's "The Funniest Office Joke, Judged Head to Head" bounty. The program writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt` — one line, 104 characters, no network, no randomness, the same line on every run. The joke turns on the reply-all: asking the whole office to stop replying all, and getting 400 reply-alls agreeing with you.
